Access Control

Role-based access control · Version 1.0 · Effective October 29, 2026 · Next review October 2027, or before anyone else is given access

This document defines who may access Tallyho's systems and consumer data, in what role, and with which permissions. Access is granted by role, never to a person directly, and each role gets only the permissions its job needs. It applies the access control policy in section 5 of the Information Security Policy.

1. Current state

Tallyho has one person, the operator, who holds every role below. No employee, contractor, vendor or other user holds any role. Separating the roles still matters: it shows which permission each task actually needs, and it is the structure anyone new would be added to. Nobody gets everything by default.

6. Granting and removing access

Before anyone other than the operator is given any role:

  1. The owner updates this document with the person, the role and the reason, giving no more than the permissions above allow.
  2. The person gets their own accounts with MFA. Accounts are never shared.
  3. Developer access uses sandbox credentials and demo data only.

When a person's role changes or they leave, the owner, on the same day:

  1. Removes every account and seat they were given.
  2. Rotates every secret they could have seen.
  3. Records the change in this document's history.

Access is reviewed at each annual policy review and whenever an account, device or credential changes.

7. Exceptions

There are no exceptions. Any exception needs the owner's written approval, recorded with a reason and an end date.
