Tallyho

A private, single-user personal finance application. It brings one person's bank, card, loan and investment accounts into one place, so he can see where his money is and where it goes.

Tallyho is not a product or a service. It can't be downloaded, signed up for or used by anyone else. The only person whose financial data it holds is its operator, Mathew Gutierrez.

What it does

  • One view of every account. Net worth, balances and history across checking, savings, credit cards, loans, brokerage and retirement accounts.
  • Spending and cash flow. Where the money went each month, recurring charges and subscriptions, and how this month compares with a normal one.
  • Planning. Budgets, savings goals, a short-range cash-flow forecast and a debt payoff planner.
  • Alerts. Large or unexpected charges, new subscriptions, price increases and low balances.

How it connects to banks

Tallyho uses Plaid to connect to financial institutions. Bank usernames and passwords are entered into Plaid Link and go to Plaid. Tallyho never sees or stores them. Tallyho receives read-only account data: balances, transactions, card and loan terms, and investment holdings. It cannot move money.

Security at a glance

Runs on one computer

All data stays on the operator's own encrypted computer. There is no Tallyho server, cloud database or hosted copy.

Not on the internet

The application can be reached only from that computer, never from the network or other websites.

Multi-factor sign-in

A password plus a one-time code from an authenticator app is required before any data is shown or a bank can be connected.

Encrypted

Full-disk encryption, plus a second layer of encryption for bank connection credentials and backups. All traffic to Plaid uses TLS.

Deleted on disconnect

Disconnecting a bank revokes its access at Plaid and deletes its data. Backups age out within two weeks.

Never sold or shared

No advertising, analytics or tracking. Data is shared only as the privacy policy describes.
